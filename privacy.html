OLI Privacy Policy

OLI is a private assistant used only by its owner (jtamdent) to manage their own Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed through Google APIs is used only to carry out the owner's own requests, is stored only on the owner's own devices, and is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Contact: via GitHub at github.com/jtamdent.
